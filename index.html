01 — About

Music first, then code.

I first busked with my saxophone in Boston when I was twelve. Eight years in, I've gone from jazz to classical, studying with Dr. Michael Hernandez and Johnny Selmer, and I also play clarinet and bass guitar and write music.

When something starts to feel comfortable, I look for the harder room: the SJSU Saxophone Ensemble, IU Jacobs, a concerto solo. Code is the newest of those rooms. I taught myself Python, learned Java, and now spend my time inside a real AI product, finding what breaks and building what's missing.
