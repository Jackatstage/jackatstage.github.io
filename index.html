Software · AI · Main project

Rigo Smart Booking System

Product development & testing · July 2026 – now

An AI that answers a local business's phone in English, Mandarin, Cantonese and Spanish, takes reservations and orders, and texts every booking to the guest and the shop. It's live with trial customers now.

Why I started it

As a college student, I order food all the time, and about 1 in 5 of those orders is takeout. Some shops take orders by phone only, with one person answering at a time: a single-server queue with finite capacity. I started out building for restaurants, but I realized most of them already have stable platforms like DoorDash and Uber Eats. So I switched my objective to shops that need reservations in general.

What I did
  • Pet-industry support. Built booking and take-order support for pet businesses, plus a dedicated test phone line to try it on.
  • L3 end-to-end smoke tests. L1 checks one piece on its own, like a single function or prompt. L2 checks that services work together, like the booking API and the text sender. My L3 tests place a real call on the test line and follow it all the way: the AI's voice, the booking saved, the texts sent.
  • Multilingual testing. Tested the call flows across the languages Rigo answers in.
  • tryrigo.com redesign. Redesigned and polished the site's UI.
  • New customer intake form. Built the application form new businesses use to sign up.
  • Tested and debugged the platform. At first I reported issues to the development team; now I fix them myself.

One bug my test calls caught: I spoke Cantonese, and it replied in English. Every line I checked started in English, and the bot replies in whatever language the session is set to, so until the session switched, it stayed in English. I made every line listen with Deepgram's multi-language mode from the first word, switch to Chinese on any Chinese text while making it harder to flip back to English, then retested with real audio in all four languages.
